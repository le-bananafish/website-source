If I’m being honest, last year wasn’t great but it wasn’t terrible either. I was burnt out and I needed a break[^1].<!-- Was a whole year necessary?  --> Did it need a whole year? Who knows but that’s what happened. I won’t make any excuses but I will make an announcement.

## What’s up?

I have been playing definitely way too much *Baldur’s Gate 3* recently; one might even say it’s been a fixation<!--hyper-obsession-->. If I’m being honest, I didn’t think I would enjoy the game so much, given I usually hate turn-based combat, don't go for high fantasy, and other role-playing games have let me down in the past (looking at you *Cyberpunk 2077*). My enjoyment has actually inspired me to revisit one of my projects and has inspired a new story based on what I currently know of and speculate about the plot of BG3 (no spoilers, please).<!-- Uncharacteristically, I am *actively* working out the plot of said story. -->

Last year Clairo’s *Charm*[^2] kept me sane and Wallice and Pom Pom Squad’s new albums[^3] were such great bops to close out the year with but I have recently discovered The Marias; why didn't anyone tell me about them sooner? At first, I actually listened to *CINEMA* on repeat, instead of their latest album *Submarine*, but both albums give me <!--cinematic vibes similar to -->Telenova[^4] vibes, who describe their music as “cinema for the ears.” Maria's voice is *amazing*.

I watched a lot of movies last year and they were kind of a mixed bag. My favourite was *Humanist Vampire Seeking Consenting Suicidal Person*, in part because it was presented in québécoise, and the worst were *Longlegs* and *Megalopolis*, though I will respect Francis Ford Coppola for sticking to his vision and financing it himself when no one else would. So far, this year I've watched *Nosferatu* which was "ummm, okay" and *Anora* which was interesting.

## Site updates

One excuse I used to not post anything to this site is the fact that I wanted to time certain site updates and changes to certain posts so that I could immediately leverage the new features. Unfortunately this just meant I procrastinated actually writing the posts and then because I couldn’t find the capacity to make the updates I accomplished nothing.

The plan going ahead is to do something considered good practice that I should've done anyways. Effectively, I'm going to work on posts and site updates separately and separate development (post drafts, ui & code changes) from production (the live site). In theory[^5], there will no longer be the bottleneck described above and there will be more "content" on a more frequent basis.

## In closing

Though I suspect I no longer have an audience, I'm grateful I ever had an audience at all. I was surprised there were actual visitors to the site beyond me.

As always thank you for reading and see you next time.
